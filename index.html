Not an official weather brief. This is a planning aid built on Open-Meteo model forecast data (updates hourly), not on official observations. Always obtain a formal pre-flight briefing from PreFlight or MetFlight GA / IFIS, and check current METAR/TAF, ARFOR, SIGMET, GRAFOR and NOTAMs before every flight. Cloud base is estimated from model cloud cover at pressure levels (the lowest layer with 25%+ cover between 950 and 700 hPa) and will differ from real reported ceilings; very low stratus or fog below ~1,000 ft may not be captured — always confirm against the METAR/TAF. Runway data is indicative — verify headings, lengths and surfaces against the AIP New Zealand. Crosswind uses the most favourable listed runway. Density altitude and freezing level are model estimates. The METAR column and home-station observation are the genuine official reading from aviationweather.gov where a station reports to the international network; most South Island fields, including Timaru, do not, and will show no live METAR rather than a guess. The local flying area uses approximate reference points, not aerodromes. Fly to your own personal minimums. © Built for South Canterbury Aero Club use.
